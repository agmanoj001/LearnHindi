# Privacy

Khel Khel Mein is a learning app for children. It is built so that **nothing about a child ever leaves the device**.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party code. The app has no links to other websites, and its security policy lets it load and contact nothing but the site it came from (`index.html`, checked by `tests/round27.test.js`).

## What stays on the device

These are kept in the browser's storage on this device only, and are never uploaded:

- each child's nickname, grade, settings, stars and progress;
- voice recordings made in the app (a family reading a story, a child telling a story back or reading a page aloud);
- the photo a grown-up chooses for the special vehicle in Vehicle Town.

## What the app downloads

Only its own files, from the site it was opened from: the app itself, its updates, its content and the story voices (the voice pack). Nothing is sent with these requests except what any web page sends to load its files.

## Microphone and photos

- The **microphone** is used only to make the recordings above, when a grown-up turns recording on or a child presses the record button. Recordings stay on the device.
- **Photos** are only read when a grown-up picks one for the special vehicle. The photo stays on the device.

## Backups

A backup is a file a grown-up saves or shares on purpose, from the grown-ups' area. It holds the progress, settings and (if chosen) recordings. It goes only where the grown-up sends it.

## The grown-ups' area

Settings, backups, recordings and anything outside play sit behind a gate: press and hold, then answer a sum written in words. A young child can't open it by accident.

## Children's privacy laws

Because nothing is collected, the app has nothing to share, sell or keep. It is designed to meet COPPA (US), GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and the families' and kids' rules of the Google Play and Apple App Store.

## Deleting everything

Clear this site's data in the browser settings (or uninstall the installed app). To remove only the recordings, use **Grown-ups › Recordings from files › Remove all recordings**.

## Changes

Any change to this statement is listed in the app's "What's new" (Grown-ups › What's new).
